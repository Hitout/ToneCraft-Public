Privacy Policy

中文版

This Privacy Policy explains how ToneCraft (the “App”) handles information when you use the Android application ToneCraft (application ID: com.gavyn.tonecraft).

1. Who We Are

The App is operated by ToneCraft. For privacy questions, contact us at gxyancn@qq.com.

2. Information We Process

1. Microphone audio

When you use the tuner, and after you grant permission, the App reads real-time PCM audio from your microphone to analyze pitch and display tuning results on your device.

  • The audio is used only for real-time, on-device analysis;
  • The App does not save the PCM audio as a file;
  • The App does not upload, sell, or share the audio with third parties;
  • When you leave the tuner, the App goes into the background, permission is lost, or an audio interruption occurs, the App stops using the microphone and clears its analysis buffer.

2. Camera and flashlight

When you enable the metronome flash feedback, the App uses the Android camera flashlight for brief flashes. The camera permission is used only to control the flashlight. The App does not take, save, upload, or read photos, videos, or camera frames.

3. Local device settings

The App stores necessary preferences locally on your device, such as language, the last-used page, tuner instrument/tuning choices, and metronome settings. These settings are not uploaded to our servers. They are normally deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

4. Vibration and audio playback

The metronome can use your device’s audio output and vibration to provide beat feedback. These features do not collect or upload personal information.

5. Information you choose to provide

If you contact us by email, we process your email address, message, and other information you choose to provide only to respond to your question, handle feedback, or respond to a privacy request. Unless a longer period is required for legal or security reasons, we retain this information only for as long as needed to handle the matter and do not use it for advertising.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

As of the last update date of this Privacy Policy, the App does not provide account registration, cloud synchronization, advertising, behavioral analytics, crash reporting, or in-app payments. During normal use of the App, we do not actively collect your name, precise location, contacts, photos, videos, or browsing history.

4. Third-Party Services

When you tap the User Agreement or Privacy Policy link in the App, your device opens the page in an external browser. The browser and GitHub may process access logs under their own privacy policies. Those activities are not controlled by the App.

5. Permission Choices

The tuner requires microphone permission, and flash feedback requires the relevant camera/flashlight system capability. You can deny or later revoke these permissions in system settings. Features that depend on a permission may not work after it is denied, while other features can continue to work.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the email address below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features, data practices, or applicable laws change. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Where appropriate, we will provide notice of material changes in the App or through other reasonable means.

8. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact gxyancn@qq.com.
